I

Research Interests

My work develops integrative approaches to understanding and treating neuropsychiatric disorders, centered on Alzheimer's disease and cognitive decline. I combine neuroimaging, brain stimulation, and computational modeling to identify neural biomarkers and test targeted interventions.

Focus   Alzheimer's disease & MCI · TMS / TBS · EEG & connectomics · diffusion MRI · machine learning for cognitive decline
