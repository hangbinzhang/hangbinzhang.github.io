I

Research Interests

My work develops integrative approaches to understanding and treating neuropsychiatric disorders, centered on Alzheimer's disease and cognitive decline. I combine multimodal neuroimaging, non-invasive brain stimulation (TMS and theta-burst), and machine learning to identify where the aging brain's networks begin to fail, and to test whether targeted stimulation can restore them toward health. Earlier work extended the same electrophysiological and network-modeling toolkit to memory suppression and addiction, giving the current program a broad grounding in how disordered circuits can be measured and modulated.

Focus   Alzheimer's disease & MCI · TMS / TBS · EEG & connectomics · diffusion MRI · machine learning for cognitive decline · memory & addiction
